## Not bundled, but worth stating

**H.264 encoding.** Screen2VMS does not ship a codec. It calls the H.264 encoder
that is part of Windows Media Foundation, so encoding runs under the licence of
the Windows installation doing the encoding. No encoder binary, and no AVC
patent-licensed code, is redistributed in `Screen2VMS.exe`.

**ffmpeg, VLC and ONVIF Device Manager.** Used during development to verify the
stream from outside the codebase. They are not dependencies, are not bundled,
and their licences do not reach this project.

**ONVIF.** Screen2VMS implements the publicly published ONVIF Profile S
specification. It is ONVIF-*compatible*, not ONVIF-certified, and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by the ONVIF organisation. "ONVIF" is a trademark of
the ONVIF organisation; it is used here descriptively.

## Regenerating this file

```powershell
dotnet list src\Screen2VMS.App\Screen2VMS.App.csproj package --include-transitive
```

Then read the `<license>` or `<licenseUrl>` element of each package's `.nuspec`
under `%USERPROFILE%\.nuget\packages\<id>\<version>\`. Any package that arrives
under a copyleft licence needs a decision before the next release, not after.
